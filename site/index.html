[ Core Capabilities ]

Forensic Capabilities

Deterministic, hardware-level forensic workflows with mathematical non-repudiation.

01 · DEFENSIVE

Media & File Sanitization

Purge physical drives, SSDs, and files aligned with NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 2 & IEEE 2883-2022 via NVMe Sanitize, ATA Secure Erase, or multi-pass CSPRNG overwriting. Emits Ed25519-signed PDF/JSON certificates.

02 · OFFENSIVE

Forensic Evidence Carving

Reconstruct deleted evidence from raw images across ext4, NTFS, FAT32, and exFAT. Evaluates structural confidence using 4-factor Shannon entropy scoring and bifragment cluster reassembly.

03 · ACQUISITION

Bit-Stream Acquisition & Cloning

Fault-tolerant raw image acquisition (.raw / .dd) and drive cloning per ISO/IEC 27037. Performs simultaneous live SHA-256 and MD5 hashing with ddrescue-style bad sector zero-filling.

SUPPORTING FEATURE

Hash-Chained Audit Ledger

Records every laboratory operation into an append-only, SHA-256 hash-chained SQLite ledger (~/.s0/s0_audit.db) for tamper-evident chain of custody verifiable in milliseconds.

SUPPORTING FEATURE

Offline Certificate Verifier

100% client-side WebCrypto validator at sector0.pages.dev/verify. Drag and drop JSON certificates, PDFs, or QR images with zero data leaving your machine.

[ Scope & Boundaries ]

What s0 Does & Does Not Do

Clarity on forensic capabilities, mathematical guarantees, and hardware physics.

What s0 Guarantees

  • Cryptographic Proof: Deterministic Ed25519 signatures over RFC 8785 canonical JSON attestations.
  • Standards Alignment: Media sanitization operations aligned with NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 2 & IEEE 2883-2022.
  • Forensic Bit-Stream Integrity: Simultaneous real-time SHA-256 and MD5 hashing during raw imaging with zero bad-sector desync.
  • Immutable Audit History: Append-only SHA-256 hash-chained SQLite ledger recording all operator parameters and target hardware serials.
  • Complete Offline Operation: Zero network dependencies; verifier and CLI operate entirely offline.

Forensic Boundaries

  • No Degaussing: Does not physically degauss magnetic platters. Mechanically failed HDDs requiring magnetic degaussing need physical degausser hardware.
  • No Overwritten Flash Recovery: Cannot recover flash memory cells once NVMe Sanitize or controller TRIM has executed (NAND physics cannot be undone).
  • Not a Live Memory Agent: s0 is a disk and media forensics suite, not an endpoint detection agent (EDR) or live RAM hook.
  • No Firmware Lock Bypass: Does not bypass hardware-locked proprietary controller firmware without authorized credentials.
  • Requires Device Authorization: Designed strictly for forensic laboratories and media you legally own or are formally authorized to examine.
