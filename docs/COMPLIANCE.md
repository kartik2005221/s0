## 2. Digital Forensics & Evidence Admissibility (ISO/IEC 27037)

Under **ISO/IEC 27037 (Guidelines for identification, collection, acquisition, and preservation of digital evidence)**:
1. **Verifiable Preservation:** Every carved artifact receives an immutable SHA-256 hash at the moment of extraction.
2. **Audit Logging:** Forensic sessions log timestamps, operator IDs, tool versions, target media hashes, and confidence scores into the append-only blockchain ledger.
3. **Non-Repudiation:** Manifests signed with Ed25519 guarantee that evidence logs were generated by authorized forensic personnel and have not been altered in transit or storage.
